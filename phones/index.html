Base iPhone 18 isn't out yet

Apple split its 2026 launch. The 18 Pro, 18 Pro Max and iPhone Duo shipped in the fall, and the base iPhone 18, the 18e and the Air 2 are expected in spring 2027. Rumored specs: a 6.3-inch display and an A20 chip on a 2nm process. With no confirmed numbers, it's left out of the charts.

Ho Chi Minh City prices are in million đồng (tr), for new 512GB phones, checked Oct 10, 2026. iPhones are official Vietnam (VN/A) models: the chart uses the CellphoneS shop price, and the tooltip also shows Apple Vietnam's list price when it differs. Google doesn't sell Pixels in Vietnam, so Pixel prices are grey imports, mostly US models, at Di Động Mỹ and Digiphone. The chart uses the lower of the two prices, and the tooltip shows both. Grey imports come with a shop warranty only, usually 12 months. Shop prices change often.

How to read the numbers. US prices are launch MSRP for 512GB. The base Pixel 10 had no 512GB option in the US, so it appears at 256GB with a tag. The Pixel 11 Pro and Pro XL 512GB prices come from retailer listings, since Google's store shows only the starting prices ($1,099 and $1,299). iPhone battery figures are for the US eSIM models; Apple doesn't publish mAh, so they come from regulatory filings. "Wired W" for iPhones is the adapter rating Apple cites for its fast-charge claim. Video hours are Apple's own ratings; Google doesn't publish an equivalent.
